The Mochiboard **code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): use it, learn from it, change it, share it, build your own thing with it.

- The name **"Mochiboard™"** (a trademark of Jessa Wolfe) and the Mochiboard logo and app icon
- **Mochi**, the pixel goo character, in any form: her drawings in the code (`web/goo.js`), the stamps (`web/stamps/`), her outfits, and any art of her
- The app's pixel art and illustrations (`web/stamps/`, `assets/`)
- The music (`web/music/`)

## What that means if you make your own version

You're welcome to build on the code. If you share or publish your version:

- **Give it a different name**, and don't present it as Mochiboard or as made by Jessa.
- **Take Mochi out**, along with the stamps, art and music, and use your own.
- Keep the copyright and license notice from [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Talking about Mochiboard (reviews, tutorials, "I made this with Mochiboard") is always fine.
