# What's new in Mochiboard

Each update gets a short line here. Mochiboard shows the new ones in Settings when an update is ready.
Write them for people, not for code: one line, what changed and why it's nice.

## November 2026 · 1.10.0

- Your files: connect folders from your computer (one at a time, or all your files at once) in Settings → General → Your files. They show under On your Mac in the left box. Disconnecting only forgets; your files never move.
- Folder cards: drag a connected folder onto a board to see what's inside, live. Double-click to open things, drag any file out to give it its own card.
- File cards: drop files in from Finder (or right-click the board → Put a file here) for a card with a real preview that opens the file with a double-click. If a file moves, the card says so and helps you find it.
- Mochi reads your files when you ask: "what's in this folder?" or "sum up this file". Read-only, only connected folders, and you can switch it off.
- Mochi connects folders for you: ask about your files and she offers a one-tap Connect button, then tries again by herself. Drag a folder onto the board (or the left box) and it connects on its own. Say "connect all my folders" and she asks once, then connects everything.
- Ask Mochi to change settings: "turn on dark mode", "hide the dot grid", "make yourself pink". She says what she changed, with an Undo.
- Play your web pages and games: an .html file card has a ▶ Play button. Mochiboard runs it on localhost and opens it in your browser, no Terminal needed, and the address stays the same each time. It only ever shares folders you've connected (a game somewhere else gets a one-tap Connect first), and only with this computer. Ask Mochi to "put my game on the board" and she makes one.
- Mochi designs in your style: ask for a start screen, menu or any screen and she looks at your real pages first (Mochiboard photographs them), reads your colors and fonts, and builds it from wireframe pieces using your own art, with a note on why she made each call.
- Video layers: an animated logo or looping character plays right on the board, straight from your folder. A transparent .webm automatically swaps for its transparent .mov twin on a Mac, so it stays see-through.
- Voice memos are written out in full now, not just the last few seconds (the Mac's recognizer starts over after every pause, so Mochi keeps each part). Deleting a memo while it plays stops it, and deleting one mid-recording lets go of the mic.
- Keep boards anywhere: Settings → General → Your boards → Choose a folder… moves them to Dropbox or iCloud Drive so they follow you to your other computers.

## November 2026 · 1.9.0

- Tables: press B (or the new table tool) for a table on the board. Type in a cell, Tab and Enter move along (Enter on the last row adds one), the little + buttons add a row or column, and right-click → Table adds or deletes rows and columns or turns the header row off. Rows pasted from a spreadsheet, a web page or a CSV arrive as a table, and a table dropped into a doc stays a table.
- Saved text styles: right-click styled text → Save text style. Styles sit under Board colors; click one to put the same font, size, weight, spacing, case and title color on whatever you've selected (or start a new note in it).
- Highlighter mode in docs: Highlight → Highlighter mode, then drag over words. Drag again to erase; Esc puts it down.
- Archive projects: right-click a project → Archive. It leaves your lists but keeps every folder and board; Archived (under your projects, and at the bottom of the Boards list) lets you look inside or restore it.
- Color your canvases: click the little dot beside a board, folder or project (or right-click → Color) to give it a color. Folders turn that color in the list and on Home.
- Tell Jessa: right-click Mochi → Send feedback to Jessa, the Feedback button in Mochi's chat, or just “tell Jessa…”. You see exactly what goes out before it sends, and you can ask to be thanked by name if your idea ships.
- Support Mochi: a little Ko-fi pill in Settings → About.
- Thank-yous: ideas from people that ship get their own line on the What's new card.

## October 2026 · 1.8.0

- Masks, the Figma way: pick a shape (or a pen line, or a cut-out) with anything under it, right-click → Mask, and it becomes the window onto them. Layers shows the mask group, you can drag things in and out of it, and there are ready-made circles, hearts, blobs and more.
- Combine shapes: Union, Subtract, Intersect and Exclude for drawn things, and Separate to take them apart again.
- A flowchart library: 14 templates with previews, from log in to bug triage, with swimlanes where there are handoffs. The Templates button sits first in the Flowchart tab, and Mochi places them offline too (“make a flowchart for checkout”).
- Flowcharts line themselves up: set a shape down near another and it connects and slides into line.
- Wireframe templates: the Templates button at the top of the Wireframing tab opens 12 common app flows (sign up, log in, forgot password, onboarding, checkout…) drawn out screen by screen, with the thinking beside each. One click adds the real, editable wireframes.
- Pictures grew up: crop inside the frame, drop shadows with a proper sheet, a stroke that works as a mat, corners that really are the corners, remove the background, drop a file on a picture to swap it, and save any picture as PNG or JPEG. Pull colors and Crop chips grow with the picture.
- Link cards show a screenshot of the page. Paste jessa.art without the https:// and it still reads as a link.
- Empty image spots show a pixel-Mochi scene that matches their label; drag a placeholder from the Wireframing pane onto any picture.
- Export as image draws only what you selected, on a transparent background, with tight edges.
- Learn: an 80-term UX dictionary and the design principle sets, inside the app, no Claude needed.
- Mochi says what she’s doing while she thinks, and does more without Claude: login flows, sitemaps from your notes, flowcharts.
- Right-click menus everywhere, lines included, with Arrange ▸ in each; arrow keys nudge; Esc closes anything.
- New stamps under New ✦: double Mochis, wings, devils and little flyers. Settings → Board → Pictures holds the picture options.

## October 2026 · 1.7.0

- The Pen: draw any shape with points and curves. Double-click any shape to pull its points, round just the corners you pick, or make a corner smooth.
- Strokes on boxes, screens and shapes: solid, dashed or dotted, with your own dash and gap. No fill leaves a clean outline in the color it had.
- Squiggly lines: turn any line wavy, zigzag or loopy, then tighten it, round it, or give it a hand-drawn wobble.
- Text, your way: type any size, set line height and letter spacing, align left, center or right, and switch between UPPERCASE, lowercase and Title Case. Titles wrap when you resize them, notes and checklists grow as you type, and Shift-drag scales text with its box.
- Line things up: pink alignment guides for everything, buttons to align and space evenly, and rotate or scale several things at once.
- Name your Mochi in Settings → Mochi, and try Calm (she sits still) or Calmest (softer colors too). Calm modes were Nikunj Tyagi's idea, thank you ♡
- Settings → Toolbar: pick small, medium or large icons; anything that doesn't fit tucks into a ··· menu. Drag the bottom of your boards list, Layers, Wireframe or Unsorted to any height.
- Mochi's music plays right from its little pill, and the colors under the color wheel come from your board.
- Send feedback right from Settings → About.
- Fixes: the text bar always shows up, aligning works on the first click, the resize corner stays on its box, text stays sharp when you zoom in, lines show up in Safari, menus never hide behind Mochi, and the Windows update banner is back.

## October 2026 · 1.6.0

- Make the sidebars yours: drag any widget by its title into a spot on either side (little boxes show where it can go). Turn widgets on and off in Settings → Widgets, where Reset layout puts everything back. The Boards panel always stays.
- Dress up Mochi: a new widget with hats, glasses, scarves, a balloon and more. Hover to try things on, click to wear them, and she wears her outfit everywhere she shows up.
- Mochi's music: a new widget with three little lo-fi songs (Strawberry Milk Beats, Cloud Desk and Jelly Jog) that loop seamlessly while you work. It never plays on its own.
- Bring to front and send to back: right-click a card, or press ⇧⌘] / ⌘] / ⌘[ / ⇧⌘[. Layers follows along.
- Click the zoom % at the bottom for Zoom in, Zoom out, 100%, Zoom to fit and quick sizes. Or press ⌘+, ⌘−, ⌘0 and ⇧1.
- Flip your camera: video cards mirror you like a selfie, or tap Flip to see the room the right way round. Your video saves just the way it looked.
- Videos can be written out too: tap the little captions button on a video, and what you said shows up underneath, ready to copy or turn into notes.
- Smoother on a Mac: hovering over notes no longer makes the board stutter, and the little Mochis use half the effort to animate.
- On Windows, Mochiboard talks about your PC, File Explorer and PowerShell, and the browser version shows Ctrl shortcuts on Windows and Linux.
- Without Claude, Mochi still writes out your voice memos and videos, and doesn't offer notes she can't make.
